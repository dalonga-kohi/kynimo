    One sentence in. A real project out.

    You stay in the chat you already use. Kynimo gives your assistant the tools a motion designer would reach for, and a way to see what it made.

    Describe

    Say what you need in plain words: the product, the length, the mood, the track.

    Build

    Your assistant assembles scenes from real components: kinetic type, logo reveals, lists, transitions. Every piece lands in a scene file, not a flat render.

    add_componentset_keyframesapply_style

    Check

    Kynimo renders frames the agent can see, then grades them: safe areas, contrast, reading time, motion on the beat. The agent fixes what fails.

    render_framescheck_quality

    Export

    MP4, WebM or GIF, plus a link your team can scrub. The project stays open for the next change.

    It checks its own work.

    An agent can't feel that a headline is cramped. It can follow a rule that says 192px. So the rules live in Kynimo, and every render comes back with a report the agent acts on.
