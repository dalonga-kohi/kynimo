## Direction contract

THESIS: A warm dark room where the work is always the brightest thing on screen. Refuses the category default of near-black + neon over a prompt box and a gallery, and refuses a literal cinema screen.

OWN-WORLD: Warm near-blacks #0C0B0A / #171513 / #221F1C, screen-white #EDE8DF, dust #8C857A, marquee yellow #F5C842 as the single light source (primary action, playhead, live state). Archivo wide/heavy display, Geist UI, Geist Mono only for timecode and scene addresses. Media resolves from ordered dither to full color; transitions open through vertical shutter slits. One-pixel hairlines, information on surfaces, no card grids.

STORY: The visitor watches an agent build editable motion, understands describe → Kynimo builds, checks itself, exports, and connects their assistant.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Huge left-weighted Archivo headline; a live, working composition (layers, timeline, quality checks streaming) fills the right two-thirds, frames resolving from dither to color; the connect-your-assistant action and prompt sit inline under the headline.
